Frequently Asked Questions

Can I upgrade or downgrade anytime?

Yes! You can change your plan at any time. Upgrades take effect immediately, and downgrades apply at the end of your billing cycle.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex), PayPal, and local Tunisian payment methods including D17 and Flouci.

Is there a free trial for Pro/Premium?

Yes! Pro and Premium plans come with a 7-day free trial. No credit card required to start. You can cancel anytime during the trial period.

What happens when I reach my monthly limit?

You'll receive a notification when you're close to your limit. You can either wait for the monthly reset (1st of each month) or upgrade to a higher tier for immediate access.

What's included in the courses?

Our courses cover technical skills (programming, data science, design), soft skills (communication, leadership), and career development. Pro members get access to 3 courses, Premium members get unlimited access to our entire library.

How does mentorship work?

Premium members get matched with industry mentors based on their career goals. You'll have monthly 1-on-1 video sessions to discuss career strategy, skill development, and job search tactics.
