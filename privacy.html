Privacy Policy & Data Rights

Last Updated: February 2026 | Compliant with Tunisian Organic Law n° 2004-63 (APDP)

🇹🇳 Local Compliance Notice

Candway adheres strictly to the Tunisian Instance Nationale de Protection des Données Personnelles (INPDP) rules. Your data is stored on secured instances and processed only for recruitment purposes within the legal framework of Organic Law n° 2004-63.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us, such as when you create an account, update your profile, or communicate with us. This may include:

  • Name, email address, and contact details.
  • Professional history, resume/CV, and skills data.
  • Payment information (processed securely by third-party providers).

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve our services.
  • Match candidates with relevant job opportunities.
  • Process transactions and send related information.
  • Send technical notices, updates, and support messages.

3. Data Sharing

We do not share your personal information with third parties except as described in this policy, such as with your consent (e.g., applying for a job) or to comply with legal obligations.

4. Data Security & Storage

We take reasonable measures to help protect information about you from loss, theft, misuse, and unauthorized access.

Storage Location: Data is processed in Tunisia and on secured AWS/Hetzner instances in France, ensuring high-speed local and international access while maintaining encryption at rest.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at privacy@candway.com.
